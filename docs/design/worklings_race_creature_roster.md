# Worklings — Race & Creature Roster

## Wildkin
Nature-first Worklings shaped by living ecosystems, plant life, and restorative magic.

1. **Moss Fox** — A fox-like familiar with moss, leaves, and subtle botanical growths.
2. **Willow Otter** — A gentle river otter with willow-like fins, leafy whiskers, and healing ripples.
3. **Petal Mothling** — A small moth-like Workling with layered petal wings and soft pollen magic.
4. **Canopy Moose** — A large forest guardian with moss-covered antlers, vines, flowers, and glowing sap markings.
5. **Canopy Elephant** — A calm sanctuary keeper with leafy ears, a moss-covered back, medicinal plants, and restorative mist.

## Elemental
Worklings whose elemental affinity is expressed directly through their anatomy.

1. **Ember Newt** — A small fire-aligned newt with glowing embers, heated markings, and flame-like gills.
2. **Tempest Ram** — A storm-aligned ram with thundercloud wool and lightning-charged horns.
3. **Gale Gryphowl** — A large, fluffy owl-gryphon shaped by wind, cloud-like feathers, and powerful air currents.
4. **Tide Serpent** — A flowing water serpent with a translucent body, bubbles, foam, and current-like fins.
5. **Geode Beetle** — An earth-and-mineral beetle with a stone carapace, crystal growths, and glowing ore seams.

## Relicborn
Powerful Worklings bonded to ancient mechanisms, rune-powered artefacts, and heavy relic armour.

1. **Key-back Pangolin** — An armoured pangolin with overlapping relic plates and a wind-up key mechanism.
2. **Vault Rhino** — A massive charger with stone-and-brass armour, engraved horns, and a relic core.
3. **Siege Komodo** — A heavy, low-built reptile with reinforced relic plating and a powerful mechanical tail.
4. **Citadel Tortoise** — A mobile fortress with a rune-powered shell, rotating mechanisms, and reinforced legs.
5. **Bristleback Porcupine** — A squat, powerful creature with relic-metal quills, steam vents, and defensive spikes.

## Bloomglass / Astralglass
Celestial Worklings formed from starlight suspended in smooth, translucent, liquid-like glass.

1. **Luminous Veil** — A serene jellyfish-like floater with a rounded glass dome and soft inner radiance.
2. **Orbitelle** — A buoyant orb-creature surrounded by halo rings, bubbles, and orbiting motes.
3. **Sea Angel** — A gentle winged floater with a fixed translucent body, soft fins, and a glowing celestial core.
4. **Moonkite** — A ribbon-like celestial glider with veil-like fins and flowing iridescent surfaces.
5. **Nautilus** — A floating shell-creature with a solid rounded body and a spiral nebula contained within its shell.
6. **Starpetal Fawn** — A delicate deer-like creature with crystal antlers and a soft opaline body.
7. **Halo Tortoise** — A small rounded guardian with frosted plates and a slow, glowing celestial core.
8. **Moonfin Axolotl** — A smooth celestial axolotl with translucent frills and a gentle healing aura.
9. **Aurora Hound** — A long-limbed crystalline companion with a haloed tail and a calm, luminous presence.

## Glitchkin
Fast, agile Worklings born from signal, interference, electricity, waves, and unstable seams between realities.

1. **Mongoose / Weasel** — A sleek ground runner with broken neon markings, afterimages, and rapid directional movement.
2. **Hummingbird** — A tiny aerial speedster with frequency-like wings and a pulsing signal core.
3. **Mantis** — An angular precision predator with fragmented limbs and glitching blade-like forearms.
4. **Dragonfly** — A high-speed aerial creature with transparent waveform wings and a segmented signal body.
5. **Raptor** — A compact predatory bird with fractured energy feathers and a fast, dive-oriented silhouette.
6. **Sparktail** — A fox-like runner with luminous waveform ears and a tail that breaks into afterimages when sprinting.
7. **Pinghopper** — A frog/gecko-like creature that leaps in short bursts and clings to surfaces near active terminals and portals.
8. **Echo Lynx** — A sleek cat-like predator whose paws leave temporary light-runes on the ground.
9. **Prism Wisp-Hare** — A rabbit-like creature with split-light whiskers and burst mobility.
